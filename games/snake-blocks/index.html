HOW TO PLAY

  • DRAG anywhere to steer your snake.
  • Push into a block and it eats one ball a tick and pays points. Break it for a big bonus. Run out of balls and the run is over.
  • Grab the gold orbs to grow, then break the biggest block you can afford. Chain breaks to multiply your score.
