HOW TO PLAY

TAP to fire. The ball hits the block in the glowing frame. Wait for the spin and the sweep to frame a good one.

Same colour blocks touching it break together. One big chunk scores far more than many small ones.

Clear the whole tower to bank its points. Spare balls pay a bonus. Run out and the tower stands: its points are lost.

STEEL never breaks. A hit wastes the ball.
BOMB clears its whole ring.
